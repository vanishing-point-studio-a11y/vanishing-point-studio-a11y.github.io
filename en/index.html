Section 5

Your choices (tracking and ads)

  • Tracking permission. When you use the App, Apple’s system may show a prompt asking whether you allow the App to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. The choice is yours. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown, but they are general ads rather than ads tailored to you.
  • Do Not Track. The App does not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals, because it is not a website. Use the iOS tracking setting above to control tracking by the App.
  • Ads are optional. You can use every feature of the App without watching an ad. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one.
Section 6

Information stored outside your country

Information collected by the ad SDK is processed by Google, which may store and process it on servers in the United States and in other countries where Google operates. This means information about you may be handled outside the country where you live. We take reasonable steps to choose providers that protect information, but we do not control how Google stores or transfers it. By using the App, you understand this.

Section 7

Children

The App is rated for ages 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will work with the ad provider to address it. Parents and guardians may wish to use their device’s Screen Time and parental control features.

Section 8

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Game data on your device stays until you uninstall the App or reset your data from the App’s settings screen. This is the only way to delete it, because we do not hold a copy.
  • Information held by Google is kept under Google’s own policies. We cannot delete it on your behalf. You can manage it through your Google account’s ad settings, and you can reset the advertising identifier on your device by changing the tracking permission.
  • Messages you send us by email are kept only as long as needed to reply and to look into the issue, and you can ask us to delete them.
